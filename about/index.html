UXBAN

// THE STORY

Blake Taylor (born April 6, 2010), known professionally as Uxban, is a rapper and producer from Hamilton, New Zealand. At 12, he began making music under the name Blakeotaylor Music — but quickly evolved into one of the younger, emerging voices in hip-hop, rap, and trap.

What started as curiosity — experimenting with sounds, recording basic demos, and learning production at home — became a deeper creative drive. He eventually rebranded to Uxban, a name that reflected the darker, sharper, more authentic direction his music was taking.

"A friend of mine was in social studies thinking of artist names and came up with Uxban. It instantly clicked — it sounded different, matched my style, and also connected to the urban environment I come from, which influences a lot of what I create."

Asked what sets him apart: "I stay true to myself. I'm not trying to copy what's trending. I talk about what I know, how I feel, and I'm always experimenting. I make everything myself, so when people hear my music, they're hearing exactly what I intended."

Uxban's sound blends heavy, atmospheric production with raw, personal lyrics. Mixing emotion and aggression, his music offers a window into the mind of a young artist navigating life, pressure, identity, and growth — one release at a time.
